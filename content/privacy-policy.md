## Overview

*Last updated: October 30, 2026*

This Privacy Policy explains what information PalBreedHub ("we", "us", "the site") at **palbreedhub.com** collects when you visit, how it is used, and the choices you have. By using the site you agree to this policy.

## Information we collect

PalBreedHub has no accounts, sign-ups or forms. We do not ask for your name, address or payment details.

- **Information you send us:** if you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in the message. We use it only to reply to you.
- **Automatically collected information:** like most websites, our hosting provider and the third-party services below may log technical details such as your IP address, browser type, device, referring page, pages visited and the time of your visit.
- **Settings stored in your browser:** the site may save simple preferences, such as your language or theme, in your browser. This stays on your device and is not sent to us.

## Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored by your browser. PalBreedHub itself does not need cookies to work, but the third-party services we use for analytics and advertising may set cookies or use similar technologies to measure visits and show ads.

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. The calculator will keep working without them.

## Google Analytics

We may use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the site, such as which pages are popular and which devices people use. Google Analytics collects information through cookies and reports it to us in aggregate; it does not tell us who you are.

You can opt out with the [Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout). Learn more in [How Google uses information from sites that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

## Advertising (Google AdSense)

We may show ads through Google AdSense to keep the site free.

- Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this site or other websites.
- Google's use of advertising cookies lets it and its partners serve ads to you based on your visits to this and other sites on the internet.
- You can opt out of personalized advertising in Google's [Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com). You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' cookies for personalized advertising at [aboutads.info](https://www.aboutads.info/choices/).

Visitors in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland will be asked for consent before personalized ads or analytics cookies are used, where the law requires it.

## How we use information

We use the information above to:

- run, maintain and secure the site;
- understand which features are useful and improve them;
- show ads that pay for the site;
- reply to messages you send us.

We do not sell your personal information.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe or the CCPA in California), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information about you, to object to or restrict how it is used, and to withdraw consent. To make a request, email us at [contact@palbreedhub.com](mailto:contact@palbreedhub.com). For data held by Google, use the links in the sections above.
