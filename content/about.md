## What PalBreedHub is

PalBreedHub is a free, fan-made **Palworld breeding calculator**. Pick two parents to see the child they breed, or pick the Pal you want and see every parent pair that breeds it, including all unique combos and gender-based results.

The site also has a full [Pal list](/pals/) with a breeding page for every Pal, so you can plan a route to any Pal without guessing.

## Why we built it

Most breeding calculators only use the breeding power formula, so they get unique combos and elemental variants wrong. PalBreedHub checks every unique combo first, then applies the same breeding power math the game uses, and shows that math on the page so you can see **why** a pair gives a certain child.

## Where our data comes from

Pal names, Paldeck numbers, elements, rarity, work suitability, breeding power and unique combos come from data extracted from the Palworld game files and published by open-source community projects:

- [palworld-atlas-data](https://github.com/Awy64/palworld-atlas-data) (MIT license)
- [palworld-knowledge](https://github.com/GGarnize/palworld-knowledge) (MIT license)

We rebuild our data when the game is updated and check it against in-game breeding results. If you find a result that doesn't match your game, please [tell us](/contact/) and we'll fix it.
