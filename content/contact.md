## Get in touch

The easiest way to reach us is by email:

**[contact@palbreedhub.com](mailto:contact@palbreedhub.com)**

We read every message and usually reply within 2–3 working days.

## What to write to us about

- **Wrong breeding result:** tell us the two parents, the child the site shows and the child you got in game. Include the gender of each parent if it matters.
- **Missing Pal or combo:** tell us the Pal name and, if you know it, where you saw it.
- **Ideas and feedback:** features you'd like, pages that are hard to use, or translations that read badly.
- **Privacy requests:** questions about your data or requests to remove it. See our [Privacy Policy](/privacy-policy/).
- **Business and partnerships:** advertising or collaboration requests.

## Before you write

Please check the [Breeding Calculator](/) and the Pal's own page in the [Pal list](/pals/) first; many questions are answered there. We can't help with game bugs, save files or account problems; for those, please contact Pocketpair support.
