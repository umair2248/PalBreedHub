## How to use this list

This page collects the most useful breeding combos in Palworld 1.0, grouped by what you need the Pal for: fighting, getting around, or working at your base. Every combo is worked out from the game's breeding rules, the same ones our [breeding calculator](/) uses, so the parents shown always give the child listed.

For each Pal you get the **easiest parent pairs**: the ones that use the most common Pals, so you can start breeding without hunting rare parents first. Open a Pal to see every other pair that works.

New to breeding? Read the [Breeding Guide](/breeding-guide/) first to learn about the Breeding Farm, Cake and how the child is picked.

## FAQ

### Are these breeding combos up to date for Palworld 1.0?

Yes. The combos are generated from the Palworld 1.0 game data every time the site is built, so they follow the current breeding power values and unique combos.

### Why do some combos need a male or female parent?

A few unique combos only work with a certain gender, for example one parent must be male. The ♂ and ♀ signs next to a parent show when that matters. Without a sign, either gender works.

### Can I breed legendary Pals?

Most legendary Pals, such as Jetragon or Paladius, can only be bred from two of the same legendary Pal. Some legendary variants, like Frostallion Noct, come from a unique combo listed on this page.
