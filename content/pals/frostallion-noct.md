Frostallion Noct is the Dark variant of Frostallion, and it comes from one special combo in Palworld 1.0: **[Frostallion](/pals/frostallion/) + [Helzephyr](/pals/helzephyr/) = Frostallion Noct**. Either parent can be the male or the female. The only other way to get one is Frostallion Noct + Frostallion Noct.

## Why Frostallion Noct is worth breeding

Frostallion Noct is a legendary Pal, one of the strongest Dark fighters in the game and a fast mount. It is not a base worker: its only work skill is Gathering 7.

Breeding it lets you choose its passive skills. Give the Frostallion and the Helzephyr the passives you want, and the egg can inherit them.

## How to breed Frostallion Noct step by step

1. Get a Frostallion (Ice) and a Helzephyr (Dark), one male and one female.
2. Place both in a Breeding Farm with a Cake.
3. Collect the egg and hatch it in an Egg Incubator.

## FAQ

### Do the parents need a certain gender?

No. Frostallion + Helzephyr gives Frostallion Noct either way, as long as one is male and one is female.

### Can the breeding power formula give Frostallion Noct?

No. It is a unique combo, so only Frostallion + Helzephyr (or two Frostallion Noct) work.
