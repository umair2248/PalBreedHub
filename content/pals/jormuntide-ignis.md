Jormuntide Ignis is the Fire variant of Jormuntide, a Dragon and Fire Pal. It can't be bred with the normal breeding power formula. Instead, it has a **unique combo: [Blazehowl](/pals/blazehowl/) + [Jormuntide](/pals/jormuntide/)**. Put those two in a Breeding Farm with a Cake and the egg will always be Jormuntide Ignis. Gender does not matter for this pair.

## Why Jormuntide Ignis is worth breeding

Jormuntide Ignis has Kindling 7, which makes it one of the strongest fire workers in the game. Kindling is used by furnaces, cooking pots and other heat-based stations, so a high-level Kindling Pal speeds up ingot smelting and food production a lot. If your base is short on metal ingots, a Jormuntide Ignis at the furnace is one of the fastest fixes.

The unique combo makes it fairly easy to get once you have the parents. Blazehowl is a Fire Pal with rarity 7, and Jormuntide is the Water form of the same Pal with rarity 8. Both are easier to get than a wild Jormuntide Ignis.

## Two ways to breed Jormuntide Ignis

- **Blazehowl + Jormuntide** – the unique combo, and the only option if you don't own a Jormuntide Ignis yet.
- **Jormuntide Ignis + Jormuntide Ignis** – once you have a male and a female, breed them together to make more and pass down good passive skills.

No other parent pair works. Because of the unique combo, a calculator that only uses breeding power would show the wrong result; this page uses the game's unique combo list.

Jormuntide Ignis has a breeding power of 170 and can breed 72 different children as a parent, listed further down this page.

## FAQ

### How do you breed Jormuntide Ignis in Palworld?

Breed Blazehowl with Jormuntide. It is a unique combo, so the result is always Jormuntide Ignis, and either Pal can be the male or the female.

### Is Jormuntide Ignis good at the furnace?

Yes. With Kindling 7 it is one of the best Pals for furnaces and cooking stations.

### Can two Jormuntide Ignis breed together?

Yes. Jormuntide Ignis + Jormuntide Ignis always gives Jormuntide Ignis, which is the best way to breed for passive skills.
