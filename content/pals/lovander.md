Lovander is a Dark Pal and one of the easiest Pals to breed in Palworld 1.0. With a breeding power of 2090, **192 parent pairs** give Lovander, and many use the first Pals you catch. The easiest pairs are [Depresso](/pals/depresso/) + [Wistella](/pals/wistella/) and [Foxparks](/pals/foxparks/) + [Wistella](/pals/wistella/).

## Why Lovander is worth breeding

Lovander is a handy early base helper with Handiwork 3, Medicine Production 3, Mining 2 and Transporting 2. It is not the best at any one job, but it covers several of them early on, including making medicine.

## Easiest ways to breed Lovander

- Depresso + Wistella
- Foxparks + Wistella
- [Dazzi](/pals/dazzi/) + [Snock](/pals/snock/)
- [Needoll](/pals/needoll/) + [Smokie](/pals/smokie/)

Every pair is listed further down this page, easiest first.

## FAQ

### Is Lovander easy to breed?

Yes. It has many parent pairs, and several use common early-game Pals.

### What can Lovander breed?

Paired with other Pals, Lovander can breed 98 different children.
