Eidrolon is a Dragon and Dark Pal with rarity 7. It has more breeding options than any other Pal in this top 10 list: 254 parent pairs produce Eidrolon. The easiest one is **[Celesdir Noct](/pals/celesdir-noct/) + [Flaracle](/pals/flaracle/)** in a Breeding Farm with a Cake.

## Why Eidrolon is worth breeding

Eidrolon has Transporting 6. Transporting moves items from workstations and the ground into your storage boxes, so a strong carrier keeps your base clean and stops resources from piling up. One good transporter can make all your other workers more useful, because what they produce actually reaches the chests.

Eidrolon is also a key step in breeding other strong Pals. It is one of the parents in the easiest pair for [Renjishi](/pals/renjishi/) (Celesdir Noct + Eidrolon), and it shows up in many pairs for [Solenne](/pals/solenne/) too.

## Best ways to breed Eidrolon

- **Celesdir Noct + Flaracle** – the easiest pair.
- **Flaracle + Solenne** – a good option if you already have Solenne.
- **Flaracle + Renjishi** – works if you have Renjishi.
- **[Roujay](/pals/roujay/) + [Silvance](/pals/silvance/)** – another choice that avoids Celesdir Noct.
- **Eidrolon + Eidrolon** – the best way to breed for passive skills once you have a pair.

Gender doesn't matter for these pairs. Eidrolon's breeding power is 300. Because it is so close to Renjishi (290) and Solenne (280), these three Pals keep showing up in each other's breeding pairs. Breed one of them and the other two are only a step away.

## Tips

Since 254 pairs produce Eidrolon, you can almost always make one from Pals you already own. Use the [reverse calculator](/#reverse) to check which of your Pals work, then pick the pair with the passive skills you want to pass on.

## FAQ

### What is the easiest way to breed Eidrolon?

Breed Celesdir Noct with Flaracle. Either Pal can be male or female.

### What is Eidrolon good for?

It has Transporting 6, so it is a strong carrier for your base. It is also a useful parent for breeding Renjishi.

### How many children can Eidrolon breed?

As a parent, Eidrolon can breed 74 different Pals, listed further down this page.
