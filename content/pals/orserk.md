Orserk is a Dragon and Electric Pal, and it has one of the strictest breeding rules in Palworld 1.0: **the only way to breed Orserk is Orserk + Orserk**. No other pair of parents produces it, so you first need to catch one male and one female Orserk, then put them together in a Breeding Farm with a Cake.

## Why Orserk is worth breeding

Orserk has Generating Electricity 8, one of the highest levels for that job. Electricity powers machines such as electric furnaces, electric crushers and other late-game production buildings, and a strong generator Pal keeps them running without you swapping workers in and out. Orserk also has Transporting 4 and Handiwork 3, so it is not useless when there is no generator to work on.

Because you can't get Orserk from any other pair, every extra Orserk has to come from your own pair. Breeding them is the only way to give them better passive skills than the wild ones you caught. Breed one pair, keep the eggs with the best passives, and use those as your next parents.

## How to breed Orserk step by step

1. Catch at least two Orserk – one male and one female.
2. Place both in a Breeding Farm at your base.
3. Put a Cake in the farm's box. Breeding stops when the Cake runs out.
4. Collect the egg and hatch it in an Egg Incubator.

Orserk has a low breeding power of 120. That means it is also a strong parent: paired with other Pals, it can breed 69 different children, listed further down this page.

## FAQ

### Can you breed Orserk with other Pals?

You can use Orserk as a parent with other Pals, but those pairs never produce Orserk. To get an Orserk egg you need Orserk + Orserk.

### What is Orserk best at?

Generating Electricity. At level 8 it is one of the best power Pals for bases that use electric machines.

### Do I need a male and female Orserk?

Yes. Breeding always needs one male and one female parent, so you need two Orserk of opposite genders.
