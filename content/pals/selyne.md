Selyne is a Dark and Neutral Pal, and in Palworld 1.0 **the only way to breed Selyne is Selyne + Selyne**. No other parents produce it, so catch a male and a female first, then put them in a Breeding Farm with a Cake.

## Why Selyne is worth breeding

Selyne is one of the best crafters in the game. It has Handiwork 7, Medicine Production 6 and Transporting 3. Put it at a workbench to finish crafting fast, or at a medicine workbench to keep medicine stocked.

Since Selyne only comes from its own pair, breeding is the only way to give it better passive skills. Keep the best eggs and use them as your next parents.

## How to breed Selyne step by step

1. Catch at least two Selyne, one male and one female.
2. Place both in a Breeding Farm at your base.
3. Put a Cake in the farm's box.
4. Collect the egg and hatch it in an Egg Incubator.

As a parent, Selyne can breed 79 different Pals with other partners.

## FAQ

### Can you breed Selyne from other Pals?

No. Only Selyne + Selyne gives a Selyne egg in Palworld 1.0.

### Is Selyne good for base work?

Yes. Handiwork 7 is one of the highest levels, and Medicine Production 6 makes it great for making medicine too.
