Lunaris is a Neutral Pal and one of the easiest Handiwork Pals to breed in Palworld 1.0. With a breeding power of 2010 it sits on the common side, so **201 different parent pairs** give a Lunaris, many of them using early-game Pals. The easiest pair is [Swee](/pals/swee/) + [Wistella](/pals/wistella/).

## Why Lunaris is worth breeding

Lunaris has Handiwork 4, a high level for a Pal you can get this early. Handiwork speeds up crafting at workbenches and building structures, so a couple of Lunaris make a real difference at an early or mid-game base. It also has Gathering 2 and Transporting 2 for small side jobs.

## Easiest ways to breed Lunaris

- [Swee](/pals/swee/) + [Wistella](/pals/wistella/)
- [Tocotoco](/pals/tocotoco/) + [Loupmoon Cryst](/pals/loupmoon-cryst/)
- [Loupmoon](/pals/loupmoon/) + [Dazzi Noct](/pals/dazzi-noct/)

Every pair is listed further down this page, easiest first.

## FAQ

### Is Lunaris good for crafting?

Yes. Handiwork 4 makes Lunaris one of the best early crafters, and it is easy to breed several of them.

### What can Lunaris breed?

Paired with other Pals, Lunaris can breed 97 different children, so it is also a useful breeding parent.
