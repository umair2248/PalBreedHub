Jormuntide is a Dragon and Water Pal with a breeding power of 590, so it sits on the rare side of breeding. Still, **205 parent pairs** give Jormuntide in Palworld 1.0. The easiest ones pair a mid-game Pal with a stronger one, for example [Fenglope Lux](/pals/fenglope-lux/) + [Celesdir Noct](/pals/celesdir-noct/) or [Frostplume](/pals/frostplume/) + [Eidrolon](/pals/eidrolon/).

## Why Jormuntide is worth breeding

Jormuntide has Watering 7, one of the highest Watering levels, which makes it a strong farm Pal. Only a few Pals, like [Shaolong](/pals/shaolong/), water faster.

## Easiest ways to breed Jormuntide

- Fenglope Lux + Celesdir Noct
- Fenglope Lux + [Solenne](/pals/solenne/)
- Fenglope Lux + [Renjishi](/pals/renjishi/)
- Frostplume + Eidrolon

Every pair is listed further down this page, easiest first.

## FAQ

### Is Jormuntide good for farming?

Yes. Watering 7 waters crops very fast, and only a few Pals have a higher level.

### What can Jormuntide breed?

Paired with other Pals, Jormuntide can breed 83 different children.
