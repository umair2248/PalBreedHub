Shadowbeak is a Dark Pal with rarity 10, and in Palworld 1.0 it can only be bred one way: **Shadowbeak + Shadowbeak**. No other pair of parents produces it. To breed it, you need a male and a female Shadowbeak, a Breeding Farm and a Cake.

## Why players want Shadowbeak

Shadowbeak is not a base worker – its only work skill is Gathering 2. Players breed it for what it does outside the base: it is a popular mount and a strong combat Pal for Dark damage. If you want a Shadowbeak with good passive skills for fighting or travel, breeding is the only way to control what it gets, since wild ones come with random passives.

Shadowbeak is also a strong parent. It has a high breeding power of 550, so pairing it with other Pals leads to many different children – 81 of them, listed further down this page. That makes a spare Shadowbeak useful even after you have the one you ride.

## How to breed Shadowbeak

1. Get two Shadowbeak, one male and one female.
2. Put them in a Breeding Farm at your base.
3. Add a Cake to the farm's box. Each egg uses up Cake, so keep a few ready.
4. Hatch the egg in an Egg Incubator. Higher rarity eggs take longer to hatch, so a heater or cooler that keeps the incubator at the right temperature helps.

## Tips for better Shadowbeak

Pick parents that already have the passive skills you want. Each egg can inherit passives from either parent, so breeding the best two Shadowbeak you own, then the best of their children, slowly builds a stronger line. Keep the Breeding Farm stocked with Cake so you don't lose time.

## FAQ

### Can you breed Shadowbeak from other Pals?

No. In Palworld 1.0 Shadowbeak is self-breed only, so the only pair that gives a Shadowbeak egg is Shadowbeak + Shadowbeak.

### Is Shadowbeak good for base work?

Not really. It only has Gathering 2. Use it as a mount, a fighter or a breeding parent instead.

### What can Shadowbeak breed?

Paired with other Pals, Shadowbeak can breed 81 different children. The full list is on this page.
