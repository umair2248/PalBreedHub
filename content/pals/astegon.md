Astegon is a Dragon and Dark Pal with rarity 9, and one of the best miners you can breed in Palworld 1.0. The easiest way to get one is **[Elgrove Cryst](/pals/elgrove-cryst/) + [Roujay](/pals/roujay/)** in a Breeding Farm with a Cake. In total, 102 parent pairs produce Astegon, so you have plenty of choices if you're missing one of these Pals.

## Why Astegon is worth breeding

Astegon has Mining 7, one of the highest Mining levels in the game. Mining is what feeds your base with ore, stone, coal and other raw materials, and a strong miner keeps every crafting chain moving. Astegon also has Handiwork 3, so it can help at workbenches when there is nothing to mine.

A wild Astegon has random passives. Breeding lets you make several of them and pick the ones with work speed passives, which makes a big difference for a mining base that runs all day.

## Best ways to breed Astegon

- **Elgrove Cryst + Roujay** – the easiest pair.
- **[Pierdon Cryst](/pals/pierdon-cryst/) + Roujay** – a good second choice.
- **Elgrove Cryst + [Univolt Cryst](/pals/univolt-cryst/)** – works without Roujay.
- **[Azurmane](/pals/azurmane/) + Celesdir** – another option if you already have Azurmane.

Gender doesn't matter for these pairs. Astegon's breeding power is 490, so the game picks it when the average of the two parents lands closest to 490. The full list of all 102 pairs is below. When you have a male and a female Astegon, Astegon + Astegon also works and is the best way to pass down good passives.

## Astegon or Anubis?

Both are popular base workers with close breeding power (490 vs 480), so some pairs land on one and some on the other. Pick Astegon if your base needs ore; pick [Anubis](/pals/anubis/) if you need a mix of crafting and mining.

## FAQ

### What is the easiest Astegon breeding combo?

Elgrove Cryst + Roujay. It is the easiest of the 102 pairs that make Astegon, and either Pal can be male or female.

### Is Astegon a good miner?

Yes. Astegon has Mining 7, which makes it one of the best mining Pals for a base.

### How many children can Astegon breed?

As a parent, Astegon can breed 82 different Pals. They are listed further down this page.
