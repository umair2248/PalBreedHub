Bulldosu is a Ground Pal with a breeding power of 1190, right in the middle of the breeding range. **192 parent pairs** give Bulldosu in Palworld 1.0. If you don't have one yet, easy pairs include [Fenglope Lux](/pals/fenglope-lux/) + [Incineram](/pals/incineram/) and [Frostplume](/pals/frostplume/) + [Shroomer](/pals/shroomer/).

## Why Bulldosu is worth breeding

Bulldosu has Mining 4 and Transporting 3. It is a solid mid-game miner that also carries the ore it digs to storage, so it fits well at a mining base before you have late-game miners like [Aegidron](/pals/aegidron/).

## Easiest ways to breed Bulldosu

- Fenglope Lux + Incineram
- Frostplume + Shroomer
- Bulldosu + [Wistella](/pals/wistella/), once you have one Bulldosu
- Bulldosu + Bulldosu

Every pair is listed further down this page, easiest first.

## FAQ

### Is Bulldosu a good miner?

Yes for the mid game. Mining 4 is a good level, and Transporting 3 means it also moves the ore it mines.

### What can Bulldosu breed?

Paired with other Pals, Bulldosu can breed 88 different children.
