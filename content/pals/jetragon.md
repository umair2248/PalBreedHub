Jetragon is a Legendary Dragon Pal with rarity 20, the highest rarity in this list. You **can't breed Jetragon from other Pals** – the only pair that gives a Jetragon egg is **Jetragon + Jetragon**. That means you need to catch one male and one female first, then put them in a Breeding Farm with a Cake.

## Why Jetragon is worth breeding

Jetragon is best known as a fast flying mount, and players use it to cross the map quickly. It also has Gathering 8, the top level for that job, so it can help with collecting at a base if you choose to put it there.

The real reason to breed Jetragon is passive skills. Wild Jetragon come with random passives, and catching more of them is slow. Once you have a pair, every egg is another chance at better passives such as speed or attack boosts, and you can keep breeding the best ones together.

## Jetragon as a parent

Jetragon has a breeding power of only 70, the lowest value among these popular Pals. Low breeding power makes it a powerful parent: paired with other Pals, the child's power is pulled towards the rarer end of the Paldeck. Jetragon can breed 70 different children this way, all listed further down this page. If you're hunting a rare Pal and can't find a pair for it, try Jetragon in the [breeding calculator](/#calculator).

## How to breed Jetragon step by step

1. Catch two Jetragon of opposite genders.
2. Put them in a Breeding Farm.
3. Add Cake to the farm's box.
4. Hatch the egg in an Egg Incubator. Legendary eggs take a long time, so keep the incubator at a comfortable temperature.

## FAQ

### Can you breed Jetragon in Palworld?

Yes, but only with another Jetragon. Jetragon + Jetragon is the only pair that produces a Jetragon egg.

### Why is Jetragon a good breeding parent?

Its breeding power is 70, which is very low. Low breeding power pulls the child towards rare Pals, so Jetragon is used to reach many rare children.

### Is Jetragon good for base work?

It has Gathering 8, so it can collect well. Most players still use it as a mount instead.
