Aegidron is a Dragon and Ground Pal with a breeding power of just 30, one of the lowest in Palworld 1.0. That makes it one of the hardest Pals to breed: every pair that gives Aegidron needs strong, rare parents. There are 101 parent pairs in total, and the easiest ones use Pals like **Tetroise Primo**, **Eidrolon Ignis** and **Bastigor**.

## Why Aegidron is worth breeding

Aegidron has Mining 8, the highest Mining level in the game. One Aegidron at a mining base keeps stone and ore coming much faster than lower-level miners. If your base runs short on ore, this is the Pal to aim for.

## Easiest ways to breed Aegidron

- [Tetroise Primo](/pals/tetroise-primo/) + [Bastigor](/pals/bastigor/)
- [Eidrolon Ignis](/pals/eidrolon-ignis/) + [Bastigor](/pals/bastigor/)
- Tetroise Primo + Aegidron, once you already have one Aegidron
- Aegidron + Aegidron, the simplest pair after you own a male and a female

All of these parents are rare, so plan for a few breeding steps first. The full list of 101 pairs is further down this page.

## FAQ

### Why is Aegidron so hard to breed?

Because its breeding power is so low (30), the two parents together need a very low average, and only rare late-game Pals have that.

### Is Aegidron good for mining?

Yes. Mining 8 is the top level, so Aegidron is one of the best miners you can put at a base.
