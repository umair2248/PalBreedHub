Bellanoir Libero is a Dark Pal and a variant of [Bellanoir](/pals/bellanoir/). In Palworld 1.0 **the only way to breed Bellanoir Libero is Bellanoir Libero + Bellanoir Libero**. No other parents produce it, so you need a male and a female first.

## Why Bellanoir Libero is worth breeding

Bellanoir Libero is a legendary-rarity Pal with strong base skills: Medicine Production 7, Handiwork 6 and Transporting 4. It is one of the best Pals for a medicine workbench and a strong crafter as well.

Because it only comes from its own pair, breeding is the only way to give it better passive skills.

## How to breed Bellanoir Libero step by step

1. Get two Bellanoir Libero, one male and one female.
2. Place both in a Breeding Farm with a Cake.
3. Collect the egg and hatch it in an Egg Incubator.

As a parent, Bellanoir Libero can breed 70 different Pals with other partners.

## FAQ

### Can Bellanoir breed Bellanoir Libero?

No. Bellanoir and Bellanoir Libero are separate Pals, and only two Bellanoir Libero give a Bellanoir Libero egg.

### What is Bellanoir Libero best at?

Medicine Production at level 7, followed by Handiwork 6.
