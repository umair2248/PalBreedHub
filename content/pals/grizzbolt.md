Grizzbolt is an Electric Pal with one strict breeding rule in Palworld 1.0: **the only way to breed Grizzbolt is Grizzbolt + Grizzbolt**. No other parents produce it, so you need to catch a male and a female first, then put them together in a Breeding Farm with a Cake.

## Why Grizzbolt is worth breeding

Grizzbolt is a strong all-round base worker. It has Generating Electricity 5 and Transporting 5, plus Handiwork 4 and Lumbering 3. One Grizzbolt can power your electric machines, carry items to storage and help with crafting, so it covers several jobs at once at a small base.

Since Grizzbolt only comes from two Grizzbolt, breeding your own pair is the only way to get better passive skills than the wild ones. Keep the eggs with the best passives and use them as your next parents.

## How to breed Grizzbolt step by step

1. Catch at least two Grizzbolt, one male and one female.
2. Place both in a Breeding Farm at your base.
3. Put a Cake in the farm's box. Breeding stops when the Cake runs out.
4. Collect the egg and hatch it in an Egg Incubator.

As a parent, Grizzbolt can breed 88 different Pals with other partners, listed further down this page.

## FAQ

### Can you breed Grizzbolt from other Pals?

No. In Palworld 1.0, Grizzbolt + Grizzbolt is the only pair that gives a Grizzbolt egg.

### What is Grizzbolt best at?

Generating Electricity and Transporting, both at level 5. It also has Handiwork 4 and Lumbering 3, so it stays useful when there is no generator to run.
