Solenne is a Dark and Neutral Pal with rarity 7, and one of the strongest crafters in Palworld 1.0. If you don't own a Solenne yet, the easiest way to breed one is **[Celesdir Noct](/pals/celesdir-noct/) + [Renjishi](/pals/renjishi/)** in a Breeding Farm with a Cake. Solenne can be made from 60 different parent pairs in total.

## Why Solenne is worth breeding

Solenne has Handiwork 8, the top level for crafting. Handiwork speeds up every workbench, assembly line and medicine station, so a Solenne at the crafting area finishes weapons, ammo and gear much faster than a weaker worker. It also has Gathering 4 and Transporting 2, so it can keep busy when there is nothing to craft.

Because crafting speed affects almost everything you build, many players breed two or three Solenne and give them work speed passives. Breeding is the only way to choose those passives, since wild Solenne come with random ones.

## Best ways to breed Solenne

- **Celesdir Noct + Renjishi** – the easiest pair if you don't have a Solenne yet.
- **Celesdir Noct + Solenne** – once you have one Solenne, this pair is the easiest of all.
- **Solenne + Solenne** – the best way to keep breeding for passive skills.
- **[Aegidron](/pals/aegidron/) + [Roujay](/pals/roujay/)** – an option that avoids Celesdir Noct.

Gender doesn't matter for these pairs. Solenne's breeding power is 280, very close to [Renjishi](/pals/renjishi/) (290) and [Eidrolon](/pals/eidrolon/) (300), so these three Pals often appear in each other's breeding pairs. If you breed one of them, you are already close to the other two.

## FAQ

### What is the best Solenne breeding combo?

Celesdir Noct + Renjishi is the easiest pair if you have no Solenne. After that, Celesdir Noct + Solenne or Solenne + Solenne works best.

### Is Solenne good for crafting?

Yes. With Handiwork 8 it is one of the best crafting Pals in the game.

### What can Solenne breed?

As a parent, Solenne can breed 73 different Pals, listed further down this page.
