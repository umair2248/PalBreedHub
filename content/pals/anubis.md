Anubis is one of the most wanted base workers in Palworld 1.0, and breeding is the most reliable way to get one. The short answer: put **[Azurmane](/pals/azurmane/) + [Roujay](/pals/roujay/)** in a Breeding Farm with a Cake and you will get an Anubis egg. That is the easiest of the 231 parent pairs that make Anubis, because neither parent is rarer than Azurmane.

## Why Anubis is worth breeding

Anubis is a Ground Pal with Handiwork 6 and Mining 6, plus Transporting 4. That mix covers three of the jobs every base needs: crafting at workbenches, breaking ore and stone, and carrying the results to storage. One Anubis can replace two or three weaker workers, which matters because your base has a limited number of Pal slots.

Wild Anubis can only be caught once, and its passive skills are random. Breeding lets you make several copies and pass down the passives you want, such as work speed skills, from the parents. If you plan to run more than one base, breeding a few Anubis early saves a lot of time later.

## Best ways to breed Anubis

- **Azurmane + Roujay** – the easiest pair, and the one most players should start with.
- **[Flaracle](/pals/flaracle/) + Roujay** – a good choice if you already have a Flaracle.
- **Azurmane + [Univolt Cryst](/pals/univolt-cryst/)** – useful when you have no Roujay yet.
- **Flaracle + Univolt Cryst** – another option that avoids Roujay.

Gender doesn't matter for any of these pairs. Anubis has a breeding power of 480, so any two parents whose average lands closest to 480 can also work; the full list below shows all 231 pairs. Once you have a male and a female Anubis, you can also breed Anubis + Anubis to keep making more with the best passives.

## FAQ

### What is the easiest way to breed Anubis in Palworld?

Pair Azurmane with Roujay in a Breeding Farm and add a Cake. It is the easiest of the 231 pairs that produce Anubis, and either parent can be male or female.

### Is Anubis good for base work?

Yes. Anubis has Handiwork 6, Mining 6 and Transporting 4, so it can craft, mine and carry items. That makes it one of the most useful single workers you can have at a base.

### Can I use Anubis as a parent?

Yes. Anubis can be paired with other Pals to breed 82 different children, which are listed further down this page.
