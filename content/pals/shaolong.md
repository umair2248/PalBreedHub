Shaolong is a Dragon and Water Pal, and in Palworld 1.0 **the only way to breed Shaolong is Shaolong + Shaolong**. No other pair of parents produces it, so you first need to catch a male and a female, then breed them together in a Breeding Farm with a Cake.

## Why Shaolong is worth breeding

Shaolong has Watering 8, the highest Watering level in the game, plus Gathering 5. That makes it one of the best Pals for a farming base: it waters crops fast and can harvest them too.

Because Shaolong only comes from its own pair, breeding is the only way to give it better passive skills than the wild ones you caught.

## How to breed Shaolong step by step

1. Catch at least two Shaolong, one male and one female.
2. Place both in a Breeding Farm at your base.
3. Put a Cake in the farm's box.
4. Collect the egg and hatch it in an Egg Incubator.

Shaolong has a very low breeding power of 40, so as a parent it can breed 69 different Pals with other partners.

## FAQ

### Can you breed Shaolong with other Pals?

You can use Shaolong as a parent, but only Shaolong + Shaolong gives a Shaolong egg.

### Is Shaolong good for farming?

Yes. Watering 8 and Gathering 5 make it one of the best farm Pals in Palworld 1.0.
