Renjishi is a Fire Pal with rarity 7, and one of the most flexible workers you can breed in Palworld 1.0. The easiest way to breed it is **[Celesdir Noct](/pals/celesdir-noct/) + [Eidrolon](/pals/eidrolon/)** in a Breeding Farm with a Cake. In total, 43 parent pairs produce Renjishi.

## Why Renjishi is worth breeding

Renjishi has four work skills: Kindling 8, Handiwork 6, Gathering 5 and Transporting 5. Kindling 8 is the top level, so it is one of the fastest Pals at a furnace or cooking pot. On top of that, Handiwork 6 makes it a solid crafter. Few Pals can switch between smelting, crafting, collecting and carrying this well, which is why Renjishi is a favourite for small bases with few Pal slots.

Breeding also lets you choose its passive skills. Wild Renjishi come with random passives, while breeding the best pair you own can give you work speed passives on every egg.

## Best ways to breed Renjishi

- **Celesdir Noct + Eidrolon** – the easiest pair.
- **Eidrolon + [Solenne](/pals/solenne/)** – a good choice if you already have both.
- **Renjishi + Solenne** – once you have one Renjishi.
- **Renjishi + Renjishi** – the best way to keep breeding for good passives.

Gender doesn't matter for these pairs. Renjishi's breeding power is 290, right between Solenne (280) and Eidrolon (300). That's why these three Pals show up in each other's breeding pairs so often – once you have one, the other two are close.

## Renjishi or Jormuntide Ignis?

Both are top fire workers. [Jormuntide Ignis](/pals/jormuntide-ignis/) has Kindling 7 and needs a unique combo, while Renjishi has Kindling 8 plus three more work skills and can come from 43 pairs. For most bases, Renjishi is the easier and more useful choice.

## FAQ

### What is the easiest way to breed Renjishi?

Breed Celesdir Noct with Eidrolon. Either Pal can be male or female.

### Is Renjishi good for the furnace?

Yes. Renjishi has Kindling 8, the highest level, so it is one of the best furnace Pals.

### How many children can Renjishi breed?

As a parent, Renjishi can breed 74 different Pals, listed further down this page.
