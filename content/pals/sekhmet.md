Sekhmet is a Ground Pal with rarity 5, and it is one of the easier Pals on this list to breed. The easiest pair is **[Frostplume](/pals/frostplume/) + [Ghangler](/pals/ghangler/)** in a Breeding Farm with a Cake. Frostplume has rarity 4 and Ghangler rarity 5, so both parents are fairly common. In total, 139 parent pairs produce Sekhmet.

## Why Sekhmet is worth breeding

Sekhmet has Handiwork 6, Mining 3 and Transporting 2. Handiwork 6 is a strong crafting level for a Pal you can get this early, so Sekhmet is a good workbench Pal before you have rarer crafters like [Anubis](/pals/anubis/) or [Solenne](/pals/solenne/). It can also help with mining and carrying when the workbenches are idle.

Because its parents are common, Sekhmet is also a cheap way to practice breeding for passive skills. Make a few eggs, keep the ones with work speed passives, and you have a reliable crafter without much effort.

## Best ways to breed Sekhmet

- **Frostplume + Ghangler** – the easiest pair.
- **Frostplume + Sekhmet** – once you have one Sekhmet.
- **Sekhmet + Sekhmet** – the best way to keep breeding for good passives.
- **[Bulldosu](/pals/bulldosu/) + [Univolt Cryst](/pals/univolt-cryst/)** – another option.

Gender doesn't matter for these pairs. Sekhmet has a high breeding power of 870, so the game picks it when the average of the two parents lands closest to 870. The full list of 139 pairs is below.

## Sekhmet as a parent

With breeding power 870, Sekhmet sits on the common side of the Paldeck. Paired with a low-power Pal, it can reach many different children – 83 in total, the most of any Pal on our top 10 list. That makes a spare Sekhmet useful when you're working towards another Pal.

## FAQ

### What is the easiest way to breed Sekhmet?

Breed Frostplume with Ghangler. Both are fairly common, and either can be male or female.

### Is Sekhmet good for crafting?

Yes. Sekhmet has Handiwork 6, which is strong for a Pal with rarity 5.

### Can Sekhmet breed itself?

Yes. Sekhmet + Sekhmet always gives Sekhmet, which is the best way to breed for passive skills.
