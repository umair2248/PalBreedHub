## How to read the breeding chart

The chart shows every breedable Pal in Palworld 1.0 against every other Pal. Pick a Pal in the left column as the first parent and a Pal in the top row as the second parent: the cell where the row and the column meet shows the child they breed. Click any cell to open that Pal's page with all of its parent pairs.

The grid is big, so use the tools above it:

- **Jump to a Pal** scrolls its row and column to the top-left corner and highlights every cell it is part of.
- **Element filter** keeps only Pals of one element on both axes, for example only Fire Pals bred with Fire Pals.
- Cells with a **gold border** are unique combos that ignore the breeding power formula.
- Cells marked **♂♀** give a different child depending on which parent is male. Hover or long-press the cell to see both results.

## How the chart is worked out

Every cell uses the same rules as our [breeding calculator](/). Two of the same Pal always give that Pal. A small list of special parent pairs always give a fixed child. For every other pair, the game averages the two parents' breeding power and picks the Pal whose breeding power is closest. The [Breeding Guide](/breeding-guide/) explains this step by step.

If you already know which Pal you want, the [Breeding Path Finder](/breeding-path/) is quicker: it shows the shortest way to breed it from the Pals you own.

## FAQ

### Is this breeding chart up to date for Palworld 1.0?

Yes. The chart is generated from the Palworld 1.0 game data every time the site is built, so it follows the current breeding power values and unique combos.

### Why is the chart split into rows and columns of the same Pals?

Breeding works both ways: Pal A + Pal B gives the same child as Pal B + Pal A. Having every Pal on both axes lets you start from either parent.

### Why are some Pals missing from the chart?

A few Pals can't be placed in the Breeding Farm at all, so they never appear as parents or children.
