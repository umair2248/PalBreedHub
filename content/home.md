## How to use the Palworld breeding calculator

1. **Breed 2 Pals:** pick a Pal in **Parent 1** and another in **Parent 2**. The child appears right away with its elements, rarity and a short **"Why this child?"** note that shows the exact math the game uses.
2. **Reverse breeding:** pick the Pal you want in the **Reverse Breeding Calculator** to see every parent pair that breeds it. Pairs that use the most common Pals are listed first, so the top of the list is usually the fastest route.
3. **Use what you own:** add a Pal you already have in **Only show pairs that include this Pal** to see only the pairs you can start right now.

Use **Swap** (the + button) to flip the parents, **Reset** to start over, or one of the **Try** examples to see a unique or gender-based combo in action.

## Breeding power explained

Every Pal has a hidden **breeding power**. Common Pals have high values and rare Pals have low values. When two different Pals breed, the game works out the child like this:

- Add both parents' breeding power, add 1, divide by 2 and round down. That's the **target**.
- The child is the Pal whose breeding power is **closest to the target**.
- If two Pals are equally close, the one with the **higher** breeding power wins.

Some Pals are never picked this way. They only come from a **unique combo** or from breeding **two of the same Pal**. That's why a formula-only calculator gets these results wrong, and why this one checks every unique combo first.

## Unique combos and elemental variants

Some parent pairs always give a specific Pal, no matter their breeding power. Most **Ignis, Noct, Cryst, Lux, Aqua and Terra** variants can only be bred this way. For example, **Relaxaurus + Sparkit** always gives **Relaxaurus Lux**.

A few unique combos also depend on **gender**: a female Katress with a male Wixen gives **Katress Ignis**, while a male Katress with a female Wixen gives **Wixen Noct**. When a result depends on gender, the calculator shows every outcome so you know which parent needs to be female.

See the full list in the **Unique Breeding Combos** section above, or open any Pal's page to see how to breed it.

## Tips for faster breeding

- **Start with what you have.** Use the owned-Pal filter in the reverse calculator so you don't chase rare parents you haven't caught.
- **Pick common parents.** The reverse calculator lists the easiest pairs first. Common Pals are quicker to catch in numbers, which makes repeat breeding cheaper.
- **Plan in steps.** If a target Pal needs a rare parent, look that parent up too and breed it first.
- **Self-breed legendaries.** Legendary Pals such as Jetragon can only come from two of the same Pal, so catch a pair before you plan around them.

## FAQ

### Is this calculator updated for Palworld 1.0?

Yes. The data comes from the Palworld 1.0 game files and covers every Pal, variant and unique combo, including the gender-based ones.

### How do I find the parents for a specific Pal?

Use the Reverse Breeding Calculator. Pick the Pal you want and you'll see every parent pair that breeds it, with the easiest pairs first. You can also open any Pal from the [Pal list](/pals/) to see its breeding combos.

### What is breeding power?

Breeding power is a hidden number every Pal has. Common Pals have high values and rare Pals have low values. The child's breeding power ends up close to the average of its parents, unless the pair is a unique combo.

### Why does the calculator explain each result?

Other calculators only show the child. The **"Why this child?"** note shows the breeding-power math, or tells you the pair is a unique combo, so you can trust the result and understand how to plan your own breeding chains.

### Why does the same pair give a different Pal than before?

Palworld 1.0 changed many breeding values and combos. Results from older calculators or guides may be out of date.

### Do the parents' genders matter?

You always need one male and one female. For almost every pair the result is the same either way, but a few unique combos, like Katress and Wixen, give a different child depending on which parent is female.

### Which Pals can't be bred?

Some Pals, like Panthalus and Astralym, can't be placed in the Breeding Farm at all. Legendary Pals such as Jetragon can only be bred by pairing two of the same Pal.
